# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **SalahMate** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, or regional school of jurisprudence (*Madhab*).

We are committed to acting and interacting in ways that uphold Islamic ethics of honesty (*Amanah*), scholarly respect (*Adab*), and kindness (*Ihsan*), fostering an open, welcoming, and collaborative open-source community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy, humility, and kindness toward other contributors and users.
* Respecting differing recognised scholarly calculation methods (e.g., Karachi, MWL, Umm al-Qura, ISNA) and juristic schools (*Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali, Hanafi*) without sectarian hostility.
* Citing primary, verifiable sources when discussing Quranic editions, Hadith gradings, or astronomical prayer calculations.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code and design feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the accuracy, privacy, and reliability of the application and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal/sectarian attacks.
* Intentionally submitting fabricated religious texts, unverified Hadith gradings, or malicious code/trackers.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub Issues or private security advisories. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
